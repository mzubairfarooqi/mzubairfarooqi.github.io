RESEARCH

Computational methods grounded in biomedical questions.

My work spans artificial intelligence in healthcare, medical image analysis, neuroimaging, computational histopathology and systems neuroscience, with a strong interest in explainability, multimodal learning and clinically meaningful decision support.

01

AI in healthcare

I develop and study computational approaches for diagnosis, prognosis and decision support using medical images and multimodal clinical data. A central goal is to move beyond accuracy alone toward systems that are interpretable, robust and suitable for clinical evaluation.

Medical image analysisExplainable AIDecision supportDeep learningTransformers

02

Brain research

My current research interests include computational neuroimaging for neurological disease, especially multimodal representations that combine complementary information from different imaging sources. I am particularly interested in early disease characterization, multimodal fusion and generalizable models.

NeuroimagingMultimodal learningNeurodegenerationParkinson’s diseaseAlzheimer’s disease

03

Computational histopathology

I have worked on AI models for tissue-image analysis and disease classification, including attention-based and transfer-learning approaches. This work focuses on extracting reliable patterns from histopathology while improving interpretability and practical usefulness.

HistopathologyAttention mechanismsTransfer learningSegmentation

04

Neuroscience

My doctoral research investigated neural circuits and whole-brain projections of the ventral midbrain in macaques. This background in experimental neuroscience informs how I frame computational questions around biological systems and clinically meaningful brain research.

Neural circuitsDopaminergic systemsVTA / SNBrain connectivityMicroscopy

CROSS-CUTTING THEMES

How the areas connect

Across these domains, I focus on explainability, multimodal integration, external validation, clinically relevant evaluation and translational potential. The long-term objective is trustworthy AI that complements biological knowledge rather than treating biomedical data as an isolated prediction task.
