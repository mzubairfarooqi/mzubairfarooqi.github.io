RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

AI that is explainable.
Research that connects.

My research focuses on developing AI technologies that use medical imaging, multimodal healthcare data, explainable AI and intelligent decision-support systems to improve early diagnosis, prognosis and treatment planning.

By combining artificial intelligence with clinically meaningful insights, I aim to develop trustworthy and deployable solutions that enhance clinical decision-making and improve patient outcomes.
