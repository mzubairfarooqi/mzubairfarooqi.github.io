BIOGRAPHY

From experimental neuroscience to clinically meaningful AI.

I am a Research Ireland funded postdoctoral researcher at the University of Galway. My academic path combines experimental neuroscience, biomedical imaging and artificial intelligence, with an emphasis on interpretable and clinically relevant computational methods.

ACADEMIC JOURNEY

Interdisciplinary by training.

I completed my PhD in Neuroscience at KU Leuven, where my research focused on neuro- and psychophysiology and the organization of dopaminergic systems. My research experience has included experimental neuroscience, stereotaxic procedures, immunohistochemistry, microscopy and quantitative analysis of ventral midbrain circuitry.

Following my doctoral work, I increasingly moved toward medical image analysis and computational diagnostics. This transition allowed me to combine biological understanding with machine learning, deep learning and explainable AI for healthcare applications.

CURRENT ROLE

Research at the University of Galway

My current work focuses on AI in healthcare and computational neuroimaging, including multimodal learning, interpretability, external validation and clinically meaningful model evaluation. I am particularly interested in neurological disease and in methods that can bridge algorithmic performance with clinical trustworthiness.
