TEACHING & SUPERVISION

Teaching computational thinking through real problems.

My teaching spans data science, computer systems, image processing and research supervision. I emphasize clear fundamentals, reproducible practice and the connection between algorithms and meaningful applications.

TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Build understanding first.

I aim to make technical material accessible without oversimplifying it. Students should understand why a method works, what assumptions it makes and how to evaluate whether it is appropriate for a real problem.

I particularly value project-based learning, reproducible experimentation and helping students connect computer science techniques to healthcare, imaging and other interdisciplinary applications.
